What is AI?

AI is a general term for technologies that reproduce or perform intelligent human behavior on a computer.

AI ده اسم واسع لتقنيات بتعمل سلوك ذكي على الكمبيوتر.

Everyday exampleWhat it does
Speech recognitionRecognises speech
Image recognitionRecognises images
TranslationChanges one language into another

Important: today’s AI is narrow — expert at one task only.

خلي بالك: AI النهارده شاطر في مهمة محددة، مش فاهم كل حاجة زي الإنسان.

Four words people mix up

These four are not separated in the textbook, but every AI example in this lesson is one of them. Getting them straight makes the rest easy.

الأربع كلمات دول مش مفصولين في الكتاب، بس كل مثال في الدرس واحد منهم. لو مسكتهم صح الباقي هيبقى سهل.

TaskThe question it answersWhere it appears
ClassificationWhat is this?Spam filter; sorting photos
DetectionWhat, and where?A self-driving car reading the road
RecognitionWhich one is it?Unlocking a phone; reading an X-ray
GenerationMake a new oneChatGPT; image-generation AI

The first three take a picture in and give a label out. Only the fourth gives a picture out — and only that one needs generative AI.

التلاتة الأولانيين بياخدوا صورة ويطلّعوا كلام. الرابع بس هو اللي بيطلّع صورة، وهو لوحده اللي محتاج generative AI.

Machine learning

Machine learning

A learning technology that makes AI work: it learns patterns from data to make predictions and judgments.

بدل ما نكتب rules بإيدنا، بيتعلم pattern من أمثلة.

1

Data

Examples are provided.

2

Pattern

The system learns a pattern.

3

Judgment

It makes a prediction or judgment.

Examples: spam filters and product recommendations.

spam filter و product recommendation مهمتين مختلفتين، لكن الاتنين بيتعلموا من data.

Prepared by: Mr. Eissa Islam

Deep learning and neural networks

Deep learning

An advanced technology within machine learning that uses neural networks and large-scale data to learn complex patterns.

Deep learning نوع متقدم جوه machine learning وبيحتاج بيانات كتير.

Neural network

A system modeled after the workings of the nerve cells of the human brain. Connected components learn from data and make complex judgments.

الـ neural network متصممة على فكرة خلايا المخ، بس هي نظام على الكمبيوتر.

Pause and think. If an AI has rarely seen something in its data, why might it struggle to judge it?

لو الحاجة نادرة في data، غالبًا الـ AI مش هيكون اتعلم نمطها كويس.

Prepared by: Mr. Eissa Islam

Generative AI

Generative AI

AI technology that uses deep learning to generate new data: text, images, audio and programs.

كلمة generate معناها يطلع حاجة جديدة: text أو image أو صوت.

Classify / predictGenerate
Spam filter sorts emailChatGPT generates text
Product recommendation predicts an interestImage AI generates an image

دي أكتر حاجة الطلبة بيلخبطوا فيها: spam filter بيصنّف، مش generative AI.

Exam warning: fluent is not always correct

Generative AI can produce text that sounds plausible but is factually incorrect: a hallucination.

لو الكلام شكله مقنع مش معناه إنه صح؛ ده ممكن يكون hallucination.

Do not use output as the answer to a school report as-is. Check it first.

استخدمه يساعدك، بس راجع المعلومة قبل ما تحطها في report.

Key takeaway

AI is the broad field; machine learning sits inside it; deep learning sits inside machine learning; generative AI is built on deep learning.

الخلاصة: AI ثم machine learning ثم deep learning ثم generative AI.

What AI is good at, and what needs caution

Good atNeeds caution
Finding and classifying patterns in complex dataEthical judgments — can carry discrimination or prejudice
Recognition and generation of images, audio and textAnything involving personal data and privacy
Probabilistic reasoning and prediction from dataFinal decision-making — who is responsible?
Biased training data makes judgments inaccurate
Hallucination, and the black-box problem

العمود الشمال بييجي سؤال اختر اللي الـ AI شاطر فيه، واليمين اختر اللي مش من دواعي الحذر. ذاكرهم مقابل بعض.
