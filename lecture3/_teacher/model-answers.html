10A farmer uses AI to identify healthy and diseased crops in photographs. Explain what it needs to learn from and one reason its judgment might be wrong.

It needs to learn from many photographs of healthy and diseased crops, so that it can find the pattern that separates them. Its judgment might be wrong if the data had too few examples, or examples that do not represent the crops it now sees — for example photos taken in different light or of a different disease.

لازم يتعلم من صور كتير لمحاصيل سليمة ومريضة، وممكن يغلط لو الصور قليلة أو مش شبه الواقع.

11Analyse the relationship between AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI. Use nested categories and give one example of each.

The four are nested categories, not four separate technologies. AI is the broad field: technologies that perform intelligent human behaviour on a computer — for example speech recognition. Machine learning sits inside AI: it learns patterns from data to make predictions and judgments — for example a spam filter. Deep learning sits inside machine learning: it uses neural networks and large amounts of data — for example image analysis for autonomous driving. Generative AI is built on deep learning and generates new data — for example ChatGPT. Each layer is more specialised than the one before it.

أربع دواير جوه بعض: الذكاء الاصطناعي ← تعلم الآلة ← التعلم العميق ← التوليدي، ومثال لكل واحد.

15A school library wants to recommend books to a student based on what they borrowed before. Explain how the AI would work, and identify one caution the school should consider.

It would use the student's borrowing record as past behaviour data, find patterns across students, and predict books the student is likely to want. One caution: borrowing history is personal data, so the school must decide who may see it — and recommendations based only on the past can narrow what a student is shown.

بيستخدم سجل الاستعارة يتوقع اللي هيعجبك، بس السجل ده بيانات شخصية لازم تتحمي.

16Analyse why AI is well suited to some tasks but requires caution in others. Refer to what AI is good at (finding patterns, recognition, prediction) and to what needs caution (ethical judgments, privacy, final decision-making).

AI is well suited to tasks that rest on data. It is good at finding and classifying patterns in complex data such as images and text, at recognising and generating images, audio and text, and at probabilistic reasoning and prediction from data — because each of these is learning a pattern from many examples. It needs caution where a pattern is not enough. Ethical judgments can carry discrimination or prejudice learned from the data. Judgments involving personal information raise privacy problems. And final decision-making needs someone who is responsible for the result, which an AI cannot be.

الذكاء الاصطناعي شاطر في الأنماط والتعرف والتوقع، بس محتاج حذر في الأخلاق والخصوصية والقرار النهائي.

17A hospital wants an image-diagnosis AI to detect disease from X-ray images. Explain why the final diagnosis should still be confirmed by a human doctor rather than left to the AI alone.

A wrong diagnosis can seriously harm a patient, so a person must be responsible for the final decision. The AI also has limits: it is a black box, so it is not clear how it reached its judgment, and if its training data was biased it may be less accurate for some patients. Final decision-making is exactly what the book says requires caution, so a doctor should confirm the result.

التشخيص الغلط بيأذي المريض، والـ AI صندوق أسود وممكن بياناته متحيزة — فالدكتور لازم يأكد.

18Generative AI can produce text that sounds plausible but is factually incorrect — a hallucination. Explain why it is dangerous to hand such output in as a school report as it is, and state what you should do instead.

A hallucination reads just as confidently as a correct answer, so neither the student nor the reader can tell it is wrong. The student, not the tool, is responsible for what they hand in, and a false fact passed on as true can mislead whoever reads the report. Instead, check every fact against the textbook or another reliable source before using it, and write the report in your own words.

الهلوسة شكلها مقنع زي الصح، وانت المسؤول عن اللي بتسلّمه — فراجع كل معلومة من مصدر موثوق.
