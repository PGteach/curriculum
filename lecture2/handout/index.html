The CPU — Central Processing Unit

This is where the processing stage from Lecture 1 actually happens. It follows your instructions one after another, extremely fast. More transistors inside it means more work per second — remember that, it comes back later in this lesson.

RAM is not storage

This is the one students mix up most, and it is worth getting right now. Watch what happens when the power goes off.

RAM — the desk you work onStorage — the cupboard
SpeedFastSlower
Power offWiped cleanStill all there
HoldsYour open apps and tabsYour photos and files
LastsOnly while the power is onPermanently

If you never saved it, it was only ever in RAM. That is why an hour of unsaved work disappears when the battery dies.

دي أكتر حاجة الطلبة بيلخبطوا فيها. لو محفظتش الملف، يبقى كان في الرام بس — وضاع. الرام مؤقتة وسريعة وبتتمسح لما الجهاز يتقفل. وحدة التخزين أبطأ بس بتحتفظ بكل حاجة.

Moore’s Law

A transistor is a tiny switch inside a chip. More switches means more computing power. Moore noticed that the number of them doubles about every two years.

AfterTransistors
Year 01
2 years2
4 years4
6 years8
20 years×1,024 more than you started with

Doubling looks small at first, then gets enormous fast. That is why a phone caught up with machines that once filled a room.

Moore never said it must happen — he just noticed that it kept happening. It is an observation, not a physical law. That distinction is examined.

الترانزستور مفتاح صغير جوه الشريحة — مفاتيح أكتر معناها قدرة أكبر. وقانون مور ملاحظة إن عددهم بيتضاعف كل سنتين تقريبًا. خلي بالك: دي ملاحظة مش قانون فيزيائي، وده اللي بيتسأل عليه في الامتحان.

Emerging technologies

Autonomous driving

Uses AI to drive a vehicle without human operation. Cameras and sensors recognise the surroundings, it makes driving decisions, and it controls the vehicle. A delay of even 0.1 seconds can cause an accident, so it uses edge computing — the decision is made instantly on the vehicle itself, not sent to the cloud for judgment.

AR — Augmented Reality

Overlays digital information on real-world images. The real world is still there; you add to it.

VR — Virtual Reality

Lets users immerse themselves in a virtual space generated by a computer. The real world is replaced.

Quantum computing

Expected to dramatically speed up computations that are difficult or impossible for traditional computers, by using the principles of quantum mechanics. A normal bit is 0 or 1; a qubit uses superposition and can hold both at once.

الواقع المعزز بيضيف معلومات رقمية فوق صور العالم الحقيقي. الواقع الافتراضي بيحط المستخدم جوه فضاء افتراضي. والقيادة الذاتية بتعتمد على الحوسبة الطرفية عشان عُشر ثانية كفاية تعمل حادثة.

Exam warning · two sentences that look right but are wrong

Both of these appear in the textbook exercises, and students pick them every year.

The wrong sentenceWhy it is wrong
✗ “VR speeds up a computer”VR has nothing to do with speed. It puts you inside a space the computer generates. The thing that speeds computation up is quantum computing.
✗ “E-commerce is paying cash in a shop”That is just ordinary shopping. E-commerce is buying and selling over the Internet. No Internet, no e-commerce.

الجملتين دول موجودين في تمارين الكتاب والطلبة بيختاروهم غلط كل سنة. الأولى: VR ملهاش علاقة بالسرعة — اللي بيسرّع هو الحوسبة الكمومية. الثانية: التجارة الإلكترونية لازم تكون عبر الإنترنت.

Key takeaway

IT developed in stages — computers, the Internet, smartphones, cloud computing.

At each stage it introduced a new technology or service and changed how society communicates, works, learns and pays.

The same four parts — CPU, RAM, storage, input/output — filled a room eighty years ago and are in your pocket now.

الخلاصة: تكنولوجيا المعلومات اتطورت على مراحل — كمبيوتر، إنترنت، موبايل، سحابة. وكل مرحلة ضافت تقنية جديدة وغيّرت طريقة تواصل المجتمع وشغله وتعلّمه ودفعه.
