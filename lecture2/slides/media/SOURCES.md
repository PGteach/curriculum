# Photo sources

The 14 photos in this folder are served from the repo rather than fetched
from Wikimedia at slide time, so the deck works on a slow or blocked school
connection. They were downloaded once from Wikimedia Commons at width 900.

All are public domain or Creative Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA); the three photo
slides in the deck carry that credit line on screen.

To refresh one, download it again from the page below and overwrite the file.
