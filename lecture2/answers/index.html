3You were writing on your laptop for an hour. The battery died before you pressed Save, and your work was gone.

1. Which part was your work sitting in?

In RAM.

الشغل كان في الرام لأنك مسيّفتوش.

2. Why did it disappear?

RAM only holds data while the power is on, so when the battery died the unsaved work was wiped. Pressing Save copies it to storage, which keeps it.

الرام بتمسح كل حاجة لما الكهربا تقطع، والـ Save هو اللي بينقل الشغل للتخزين.

10In autonomous driving, why is it necessary to process data instantly on the vehicle side using edge computing, rather than sending the data to the cloud for judgment? Explain your answer.

Because a delay of even 0.1 seconds can lead to an accident. Sending the data to the cloud and waiting for the answer takes too long, so the decision must be made instantly on the vehicle itself, using edge computing.

جزء من الثانية ممكن يعمل حادثة، فلازم القرار يتاخد في العربية نفسها مش يستنى السحابة.

12Analyse how the spread of cloud computing (from the 2010s onward) has changed the way information technology is used. In your answer, refer to: large-scale data analysis, AI, and "IT as a service".

Cloud computing spread from the 2010s onward. Because storage and processing power can be rented over the Internet instead of owned, it made large-scale data analysis possible. That data and computing power in turn support AI, which needs both. It also changed IT into something delivered as a service over the Internet, rather than hardware each organisation must buy and maintain — so smaller organisations and individuals can now use computing power that once needed a large investment.

السحابة خلّت تحليل البيانات الضخمة ممكن، وده بيدعم الذكاء الاصطناعي، وخلّت التكنولوجيا خدمة بتأجّرها بدل ما تشتري أجهزة.
