AI helps decide things about people

AI now helps make real decisions about people: selecting candidates for job interviews, identifying individuals through face recognition, and determining how personal data is used. If the data it learned from was biased, it can be unfair — and it can be hard to know why it decided at all.

الـ AI بيساعد يقرر مين ياخد انترفيو، والكاميرا تقول انت مين، وبياناتك تتستخدم إزاي. لو الداتا متحيزة ممكن يبقى ظالم، وساعات مش هتعرف قرر كده ليه.

Part 1 · Algorithmic bias

Algorithmic bias

Bias in AI judgments caused by bias in the training data.

التحيز الخوارزمي: الـ AI بيحكم بشكل متحيز لأن الداتا اللي اتدرب عليها كانت متحيزة.

Example from the bookWho is treated unfairly
A hiring AIUnfairly evaluates applicants of a particular gender
A face-recognition AIIs more likely to misidentify particular ethnic groups

Two places bias gets in

In the training dataIn the way the system is built
Insufficient data on certain attributesAn inappropriate choice of variables
Past discriminatory tendencies reflected in the dataA proxy variable that indirectly stands in for a protected attribute
The design of the model itself

التحيز بيدخل من الداتا (داتا قليلة عن فئات، أو تمييز قديم جوه الداتا) أو من طريقة بناء النظام (متغيرات غلط، متغير بديل، أو تصميم النموذج).

Prepared by: Mr. Eissa Islam

A proxy variable

A protected attribute is something a decision must not discriminate on — gender, or ethnic group. Removing it from the data is not enough. A hiring AI is never told an applicant’s gender, but it is given another detail that goes closely with gender: it can still learn the old bias through that detail, which is standing in for gender.

لو شلت النوع من الداتا مش كفاية: لو فيه متغير تاني مرتبط بيه أوي، الـ AI هيتعلم التحيز من خلاله. ده الـ proxy variable.

Think it through. A hiring AI was found to rate applicants of one gender lower. What is the most likely cause — and one measure to fix it?

AI توظيف بيقيّم نوع معين أقل. إيه السبب الأرجح؟ واقترح إجراء واحد يصلّحه.

Part 3 · Explainable AI and responsibility

Explainable AI (XAI)

Technology that makes it possible for humans to understand why an AI made a particular judgment.

الـ XAI: تقنية بتخلي البني آدم يفهم الـ AI أخد الحكم ده ليه.

When the decision-making process is opaque — a black box — it is difficult to verify whether the result is correct. So “the answer is right, so the process does not matter” is false.

It got it wrong. Who answers?

المطوّر والمشغّل والمستخدم كل واحد شايفها بشكل، ولسه مفيش معيار واضح متفق عليه.

Prepared by: Mr. Eissa Islam

Responsibility is not accountability

ResponsibilityAccountability
The roles and duties of each partyBeing answerable for the AI’s decisions
Developer, operator, user — who does whatKnowing who can be held to account, according to their role
Who answers when an AI judgment is wrongOne of the four principles of AI ethics

المسؤولية = أدوار وواجبات كل طرف. المساءلة = إن فيه حد يتحاسب فعلاً حسب دوره، ودي من المبادئ الأربعة. الوزارة بتسأل على الفرق ده.

Part 4 · The four principles of AI ethics

Fairness

Not unjustly discriminating against any particular person or group

Transparency

Showing the AI’s decision-making process and inner workings clearly

Privacy protection

Handling personal information appropriately and protecting privacy

Accountability

Being answerable for the AI’s decisions

احفظهم بالتعريف: العدالة، الشفافية، حماية الخصوصية، المساءلة.

Which principle is it?

SituationPrinciple
A hiring AI evaluates fairly regardless of genderFairness
The decision-making process is disclosed to users in an easy-to-understand wayTransparency
Collected personal data is not used for other purposes than originally intendedPrivacy protection
Someone is answerable when an AI’s diagnostic result turns out to be wrongAccountability

Exam warning

Looks right, but is falseWhy
Even if an AI’s process is opaque, there is no problem as long as the result is correct.An opaque result cannot be verified.
Who is responsible when an AI is wrong has already been clearly determined.Views differ; no clear standard exists yet.
Algorithmic bias is caused by the AI’s processing speed.It comes from bias in the training data.

Think as an engineer

A company wants to use a hiring AI. Investigate one documented case of an AI accused of bias — what group did it affect, and what data might have caused it? Consider responsibility: the developer, the company using it, or the operator. Decide one rule the company must follow before using it, justified with the principles.

دوّر على حالة حقيقية، حدد مسؤولية كل طرف، واقترح قاعدة واحدة وبرّرها بالمبادئ.

In a new context

A school wants face-recognition cameras at its gate to record attendance automatically. Identify one practical benefit and one privacy concern. If the camera misidentifies a student, who should be responsible — the school, or the company that made the AI? Give a reason.

فايدة واحدة، ومشكلة خصوصية واحدة، ولو الكاميرا غلطت مين المسؤول وليه.

Key takeaway · accurate is not enough

If training data is biased, an AI can reproduce that bias. Using AI responsibly means checking for bias, being able to explain decisions, and knowing who is accountable — guided by fairness, transparency, privacy protection and accountability.

الداتا المتحيزة بتطلّع AI متحيز. الاستخدام المسؤول = نراجع التحيز، ونشرح القرار، ونعرف مين يتحاسب.
