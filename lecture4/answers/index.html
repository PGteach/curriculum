3Answer the following questions.

1. What is training data?

Training data is the set of examples an AI system learns its patterns from before it is used.

البيانات اللي الـ AI بيتعلم منها.

2. How does too little data on a group affect it?

With too little data on a group, the AI learns that group's pattern poorly, so its judgments about that group are less accurate — it misjudges them more often.

لو بيانات مجموعة قليلة، هيغلط معاها أكتر.

4Explain the main causes of algorithmic bias. Give one cause that comes from the training data and one that comes from the way the system is built.

A cause in the training data

Insufficient data on certain attributes, or past discriminatory tendencies reflected in the data. The AI learns whatever pattern is in the data, so it repeats that bias.

بيانات مش كفاية، أو بيانات فيها تمييز قديم.

A cause in the way the system is built

An inappropriate choice of variables, a proxy variable that indirectly stands in for a protected attribute, or the design of the model itself.

متغيرات غلط، أو متغير بديل، أو تصميم النموذج.

6Answer about explainable AI.

1. What is XAI?

Explainable AI is technology that makes it possible for humans to understand why an AI made a particular judgment.

تقنية بتخلينا نفهم الـ AI قرر ليه.

2. Why is a black box a problem even when the result is correct?

If the process is opaque, nobody can verify whether the result is correct. A right answer reached by chance cannot be told apart from a wrong one, and an unfair decision cannot be found or challenged.

من غير ما نشوف الأسباب، مش هنقدر نتأكد ولا نكتشف الظلم.

7Answer about responsibility and accountability.

1. The three parties

The developer, the user and the operator.

المطوّر والمستخدم والمشغّل.

2. Responsibility compared with accountability

Responsibility is the roles and duties of each party — who does what, and who answers when a judgment is wrong. Accountability is being answerable for the AI's decisions: knowing who can be held to account, according to those roles. Accountability is one of the four principles of AI ethics.

المسؤولية: أدوار وواجبات كل طرف. المساءلة: مين يتحاسب على القرار حسب دوره.

9Complete the table: what each principle means, and one situation where it applies.

Fairness

Not unjustly discriminating against any person or group — e.g. a hiring AI that evaluates fairly regardless of gender.

Transparency

Showing the decision-making process and inner workings clearly — e.g. telling users in plain language how the AI decides.

Privacy protection

Handling personal information appropriately — e.g. not using collected data for another purpose.

Accountability

Being answerable for the AI's decisions — e.g. someone answers when a diagnostic result is wrong.

المبادئ الأربعة لازم تتحفظ بالتعريف.

10Analyze how algorithmic bias can arise in an AI system, and how it can be reduced. In your answer, refer to the cause of bias (biased or insufficient training data) and to how the principles of fairness and transparency help address it.

Algorithmic bias arises because an AI learns its patterns from training data. If that data is biased — for example it contains too little data on certain groups, or it reflects past discrimination — the AI reproduces the same bias in its judgments. Bias can also enter through the way the system is built, such as a proxy variable that stands in for a protected attribute. It can be reduced by checking the training data for missing or unfair patterns and testing the AI's results across different groups, which applies the principle of fairness; and by making the decision-making process visible, for example with explainable AI, which applies the principle of transparency and lets people find and correct unfair decisions.

السبب: البيانات. العلاج: نراجع البيانات ونختبر النتايج (عدالة)، ونوضّح طريقة القرار (شفافية).

11A hiring AI was found to rate applicants of a particular gender lower. Using algorithmic bias and the principles of AI ethics, explain the most likely cause and one possible measure to address it.

One possible answer. The most likely cause is that the training data reflected past bias — for example, the company's past hiring decisions favoured one gender, or there was too little data on the other. One measure is to check the training data and test the AI's results for each gender before using it, which applies the principle of fairness.

مثال لإجابة: السبب بيانات قديمة متحيزة، والإجراء مربوط بمبدأ.

12A school wants to use face-recognition cameras at its gate to record attendance automatically. Identify one practical benefit and one privacy concern. If the camera misidentifies a student, who should be responsible — the school, or the company that made the AI? Give a reason.

One possible answer. Benefit: attendance is recorded automatically and quickly, which saves teachers' time. Privacy concern: students' faces are personal data, and they could be tracked or the data used for another purpose. Responsibility: the school, because it chose to use the system and operates it every day — though it is also reasonable to say the company, because it built the AI and chose its data. What matters is the reason.

الإجابتين صح في المسؤولية — المهم السبب.

19Find one documented case where an AI system was accused of being unfair or biased. Record the source (a news report, study or official statement). What group did it affect, and what data might have caused it?

This is a research task, so there is no single answer. A good answer names a real, checkable source, says which group was affected, and links the cause to biased or insufficient training data — for example, Reuters reported in 2018 that Amazon's hiring AI marked down CVs containing the word "women's", because it learned from ten years of mostly male CVs.

One possible answer. Use: hiring. Rule: before the AI is used, its results must be tested for each gender and age group, and any difference must be explained and corrected. Principle: fairness — no group may be unjustly discriminated against.
