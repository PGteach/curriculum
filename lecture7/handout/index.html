A rushed reaction makes it worse

Even a well-protected system can be attacked: data leaked, files tampered with, a service knocked offline. A rushed, unplanned reaction only makes the damage worse. Organizations follow a clear, ordered response so that damage is contained, the cause is removed, and the same problem does not return — and they decide in advance which risks to guard against first, because no team can fix everything at once.

Today’s question. When a security incident happens, how should an organization respond in order — and how does it decide which risks to address first?

السؤال الرئيسي: لما يحصل حادث أمني، المؤسسة تنظّم استجابتها إزاي بالترتيب، وتقرر أنهي مخاطر تعالجها الأول؟

Explore · in pairs. A school computer is infected with malware that is spreading. Put these in order: restore the files from a backup · unplug it from the network · find out how it got in · remove the malware.

Part 1 · What is a security incident?

Security incident

A situation in which actual damage occurs due to an information security problem.

الحادث الأمني: حدث بيأثر على سرية المعلومات أو سلامتها أو توافرها، أو بيهددها بجدية، أو بيخالف سياسة أمنية.

TypeDescription
Information leakPersonal or confidential information is leaked to the outside
Data tamperingData within a system is altered without authorization
Service outageA service becomes unavailable due to attacks on the server, etc.
Is it a security incident?Answer
Data was encrypted by a ransomware infectionYes
Customer information was leaked through unauthorized accessYes — an information leak
A software update was performed to add new featuresNo — nothing was damaged
A web service became unavailable due to an attack on the serverYes — a service outage

Part 2 · Six stages, in order

Prepared by: Mr. Eissa Islam
StageWhat happens
1 PreparationEstablish response procedures and structures in advance, in preparation for incidents
2 DetectionDiscover and confirm that an incident has occurred
3 ContainmentLimit the scope of impact so that damage does not spread further
4 EradicationRemove the cause of the incident (e.g. remove malware)
5 RecoveryReturn systems and services to their normal state (may restore from a clean backup)
6 Prevention of recurrenceAnalyze the cause and take measures so that the same incident does not occur again

التحضير ← الاكتشاف ← الاحتواء ← إزالة التهديد (الاستئصال) ← الاستعادة ← الدروس المستفادة والتحسين.

Stage 6 has two names. The English book says prevention of recurrence; the Arabic book and the ministry say lessons learned and improvement — analyze what happened, document the lessons, improve the controls. Learn both.

المرحلة السادسة: "منع التكرار" = "الدروس المستفادة والتحسين".

Contain first, then remove the cause

Containment · eradication

Containment limits the spread of damage; eradication removes the cause. Containment comes first. If recovery is performed without removing the cause, the same incident may occur again.

الاحتواء: الحد من انتشار الضرر. الاستئصال: إزالة السبب. الاحتواء الأول.

Prepared by: Mr. Eissa Islam

Pause and think. Why must containment come before eradication in the six-stage response?

ليه الاحتواء لازم ييجي قبل الاستئصال؟

The order is a guide, not a cage

The six stages give the general sequence, but in a real response some activities can overlap or repeat. For example, recovery can reveal traces of the threat that are still there — and the team goes back to containment or eradication.

الاستجابة الفعلية ممكن يحصل فيها تداخل أو تكرار؛ الاسترداد ممكن يكشف بقايا للتهديد فنرجع للاحتواء أو إزالة التهديد.

Which stage is it?

ActionStage
Establishing response procedures in advancePreparation
Discovering and confirming that an incident has occurredDetection
Disconnecting a malware-infected computer from the networkContainment
Removing the malware infectionEradication
Restoring a clean system from a backupRecovery
Analyzing the cause so it does not occur againPrevention of recurrence

Why must the six stages go in order? If recovery is done before eradication, the system is back but the cause is still there, so the same incident can happen again. Eradicate first, and recovery returns a clean system.

لو رجّعنا النظام قبل ما نشيل السبب، الحادث ممكن يتكرر.

Part 3 · Risk = impact × likelihood

Risk

The possibility of an information security problem occurring in the future, and its impact.

الخطر: احتمال إن يحصل حدث بيسبب ضرر، مع مراعاة احتمال وقوعه وحجم أثره.

FactorMeaning
ImpactHow much damage occurs if the risk actually materializes
LikelihoodHow high the probability is that the risk will actually materialize

Risks with both high impact and high likelihood are addressed first; it is difficult to address every risk at the same time, so priorities are set. High impact with low likelihood (3 × 1 = 3) is usually classed as medium.

التأثير العالي والاحتمالية العالية أعلى أولوية؛ ومش هنقدر نعالج كل المخاطر في نفس الوقت.

Looks right, but is falseWhy
The size of a risk can be determined by impact alone.Impact and likelihood, multiplied.
It is enough to address only high-impact, or only high-likelihood, risks.Both factors count.
All risks must be addressed simultaneously.Set priorities.
Detection → Eradication → Containment → Recovery.Containment comes before eradication.

تلميح: لو رجّعت قبل ما تشيل السبب، البرمجية الخبيثة لسه موجودة.

In a new context

An online shop has just detected that customer data has been leaked. Name the next two stages it should carry out and why, and identify one risk it should prioritize afterward.

حدد المرحلتين الجايين وليه، وخطر واحد ياخد الأولوية بعد كده.

Key takeaway · respond in order, fix the worst first

Follow the six stages in order (prepare → detect → contain → eradicate → recover → prevent), and prioritize risks by impact × likelihood.

اتبع المراحل الست بالترتيب مع إمكانية الرجوع لمرحلة سابقة، وحدد أولويات المخاطر بالتأثير × الاحتمالية.
