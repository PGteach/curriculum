2Answer the following questions.

1. Security incident and examples

A situation in which actual damage occurs due to an information security problem (the Arabic book: an event that affects the confidentiality, integrity or availability of information, or violates a security policy). Examples: an information leak, data tampering, a service outage.

حادث فيه ضرر فعلي — تسرب، تلاعب، انقطاع خدمة.

2. The six stages; preparation and detection

Preparation, detection, containment, eradication, recovery, prevention of recurrence (lessons learned and improvement). Preparation sets up procedures and structures in advance, so the team is ready; detection discovers and confirms that an incident has happened.

التحضير عشان نبقى جاهزين، والاكتشاف عشان نتأكد إن فيه حادث.

4Answer the following questions.

1. Recovery; lessons learned and improvement

Recovery returns systems and services to a safe, normal state, which may include restoring from a clean backup. Lessons learned and improvement analyzes what happened, documents the lessons and improves the controls, so the incident is less likely to happen again.

الاستعادة ترجّع النظام، والدروس المستفادة تمنع التكرار.

2. Why containment before eradication

Containment stops the damage spreading while the cause is still active. If eradication came first, the damage could keep spreading during it, to more machines or data — and the response would have to chase it.

لو عكسنا، الضرر هيفضل ينتشر.

5Why must containment come before eradication in the six-stage response?

Containment limits the spread of the damage. While the cause is being removed, it could keep spreading — for example, malware moving to other computers on the network. So the infected part is isolated first, and then the cause is removed safely.

اعزل الأول، وبعدين شيل السبب بأمان.

6Explain why the six stages of incident response must be carried out in order, using recovery and eradication as an example. Refer to what happens if the cause is not removed.

Each stage depends on the one before it. Containment comes first, to stop the damage spreading; eradication then removes the cause, such as the malware; only then does recovery return the systems to normal. If recovery is done before eradication, the systems are restored while the cause is still there — the malware or the weakness it used — so the same incident can happen again, and the work has to be repeated. Following the order keeps the damage small and stops the incident returning. In practice some stages can overlap: recovery may reveal traces of the threat, and the team goes back to containment or eradication.

لو رجّعنا النظام قبل ما نشيل السبب، البرمجية لسه موجودة والحادث ممكن يتكرر.

8Answer the following questions.

1. Containment and eradication

Containment limits the scope of the impact so the damage does not spread further (e.g. disconnecting an infected computer). Eradication removes the cause of the incident (e.g. removing the malware).

الاحتواء يوقف الانتشار، والاستئصال يشيل السبب.

2. How risk size is estimated

Risk size = impact × likelihood. Impact: how much damage if the risk happens. Likelihood: how probable it is that it happens.

درجة الخطر = التأثير × الاحتمالية.

9Answer the following questions.

1. The risk evaluation matrix

Each risk is given an impact score and a likelihood score, and its size is impact × likelihood. Risks with both high impact and high likelihood are the highest priority and are addressed first; the others are ranked by their scores.

التأثير العالي والاحتمالية العالية أعلى أولوية.

11Discuss the importance of the lessons-learned-and-improvement stage, and how it helps prevent security incidents from happening again.

Lessons learned and improvement is the last stage. The team analyzes what happened and how the attacker got in, documents the lessons, and improves the controls — for example patching the weakness, adding a protection, or changing a procedure. Without it, the same weakness stays and the same incident can return. With it, the incident becomes less likely to happen again, and its effect smaller if it does.

من غير المرحلة دي، نفس الثغرة بتفضل والحادث يتكرر.

12Explain how the recovery stage and the lessons-learned-and-improvement stage are carried out when dealing with a ransomware attack.

Recovery: once the ransomware has been removed, the encrypted systems are restored to a safe, normal state from a clean backup, and checked before they are used again. Lessons learned and improvement: the team finds how the ransomware got in, documents it, and improves the controls — for example updating the software it exploited, training staff, and keeping regular backups — so it does not happen again.

استعادة من نسخة احتياطية سليمة، وبعدين نعرف دخلت إزاي ونحسّن الحماية.

13An online shop has just detected that customer data has been leaked. Using the six-stage response and risk assessment, name the next two stages it should carry out and why, and identify one risk it should prioritize afterward.

One possible answer. Next stage: containment — cut off the route the data is leaking through, so no more customer data leaves. Then eradication — remove the cause, for example close the weakness the attacker used or remove the malware. Afterward, one risk to prioritize: customers' leaked passwords being used to log in to their accounts — high impact and, now that the data is out, high likelihood — so it comes first.

مثال لإجابة: الاحتواء ثم الاستئصال بالأسباب، وخطر واحد بالتأثير × الاحتمالية.

14Think as an engineer. A school's student-records system is hit by ransomware: files are encrypted and staff cannot log in, and the incident has just been detected. List the stages still to carry out and what each means here; compare two risks, (i) another ransomware attack and (ii) a brief power cut, by impact and likelihood; and recommend the first three actions, with one reason why order matters.

One possible answer. Still to do: containment (disconnect the infected computers so the ransomware cannot spread), eradication (remove the ransomware and close the weakness it used), recovery (restore the records from a clean backup), and prevention of recurrence (find how it got in, document it, improve the controls). Risks: another ransomware attack has large impact and, until the weakness is fixed, high likelihood; a brief power cut has smaller impact. So ransomware comes first. First three actions: isolate the infected computers, remove the ransomware, then restore from the backup — because restoring before the cause is removed would let the ransomware encrypt the files again.

مثال لإجابة: المراحل الباقية، ومقارنة المخاطر، وأول تلات إجراءات بالسبب.

15Reflect: which stage of incident response do you think is hardest to do well under pressure, and why?

One possible answer. Containment, because it has to be decided fast while the damage is still spreading, and disconnecting systems also stops people working — under pressure it is tempting to skip it and go straight to recovery.

أي مرحلة، بسبب مقنع.

16Challenge: suggest one thing an organization could prepare in advance (stage 1) that would make the other stages faster.

One possible answer. A written response plan that says who does what and how to isolate each system, with regular, tested backups — then containment and recovery can start at once instead of being worked out during the incident.

أي حاجة مسبقة، مع إزاي بتسرّع مرحلة تانية.
